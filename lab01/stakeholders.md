# Stakeholders: priority and veto

S01 (Head of the IT department) and S02 (Tal Tongsreng, project manager) are the two stakeholders with high influence and high interest, so they get the PM's attention first: the sponsor controls the charter, the 200 USD cash cap and every phase-gate decision, and the PM owns the 490 person-hour plan and the week-7 MVP and week-14 pilot commitments, so both need a standing weekly one-to-one and signed minutes at the week-1, week-7 and week-13 gates.

The veto stakeholder is S09, the data protection officer, because the week-14 pilot cannot run without approval of how student names and results are stored on campus, and that approval is an explicit week-13 readiness item.
