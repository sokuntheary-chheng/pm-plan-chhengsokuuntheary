## What PRINCE2 would add that this governance lacks

PRINCE2 would add a business case that is re-validated at every stage boundary: our four gates check plan, hours, coverage, latency and defects, but nothing in governance.md asks whether the project is still worth doing. Concretely, at G2 (2026-11-16) PRINCE2 would require the executive to re-confirm the expected benefit, for example the instructor marking hour saved per week and the 50-student pilot justification from the charter, and to close the project early if that number no longer holds. PRINCE2 would also add a defined change control approach with a named change authority and a change budget, and a benefits review after the pilot, none of which exists in our scheme: our only change control is the charter rule that scope changes need sponsor approval within 5 working days.

## What PRINCE2 would make heavier

PRINCE2 would make the project heavier in exactly the places where a four-student team has no spare capacity: management stages would have to be defined in advance with a plan per stage, and each of our four gates would become a stage boundary producing an updated business case, a stage boundary report and a plan for the next stage, which is roughly four extra documents written by students who have 10 h per week. The project board would add formal roles (executive, senior user, senior supplier) with scheduled meetings, and six tolerance areas (time, cost, quality, scope, risk, benefit) reported instead of our five, while the change authority would slow down the sprint-by-sprint re-ordering of the backlog that the hybrid depends on to absorb the unspecified screens.
