# Negotiation · sponsor asks for all Should features by week 10

The dialogue below is a simulated role-play using the challenge's W14-pilot scenario; it does not record an actual sponsor conversation or decide the separate CR-03 W12 case.

## Data sheet (week 4)

| Measure | Value |
| ------- | ----- |
| Capacity W5-W10 | 6 × 40 = 240 h |
| Must baseline / completed after two sprints | 300 h / 148 h |
| Must remaining | 300 − 148 = 152 h |
| Should total (Task 2 rating) | 110 h |
| Needed by W10 = Must remaining + all Should | 152 + 110 = 262 h |
| Shortfall by W10 | 262 − 240 = 22 h = 0.55 weeks; 0 h contingency used |
| Shortfall if the 56 h contingency must remain intact | 22 + 56 = 78 h = 1.95 weeks |
| Velocity, sprints 1-2 (points done) | 18, 21 → 19.5 average points |
| Project baseline | 504 h planned work + 56 h contingency; 200 USD cash cap; 12 USD per paid hour |

## Script

**Sponsor:** I need every Should feature demonstrable at the open day in week 10. It is mostly small stuff.

**PM:** Small or not, the remaining 152 h of Must work plus 110 h of Should work is 262 h against 240 h of W5-W10 capacity, a 22 h shortfall.

**Sponsor:** It is a small change; add the reporting dashboard too.

**PM:** The Should list is already 110 h; after the 152 h of Must work, only 88 h of the 240 h capacity remains for Should work, so the gap is 22 h.

**Sponsor:** Work a bit harder; the team can make up 22 hours.

**PM:** Capacity is 40 h per week, or 240 h across W5-W10; covering the 22 h gap from the 56 h contingency would leave 34 h of contingency.

**Sponsor:** Other teams manage all their features before a demonstration.

**PM:** Our first two sprint results were 18 and 21 points, averaging 19.5 points; that velocity does not change the 240 h capacity or the 22 h gap.

**Sponsor:** Decide later; keep the entire list in the plan for now.

**PM:** Keeping the 56 h contingency intact makes the W10 capacity gap 78 h, or 1.95 weeks, for the 262 h Must-plus-Should commitment.

**Sponsor:** I need a credible open-day demo, not another schedule report.

**PM:** Option 1 completes F16 (30 h) and F11 (24 h), the two largest Should items: 152 + 30 + 24 = 206 h, leaving 34 h of W5-W10 capacity.

**Sponsor:** What happens to the other Should items?

**PM:** F09 (20 h), F12 (12 h) and F13 (24 h) total 56 h and remain scheduled for the week-14 pilot; all 110 h of Should work stay in the baseline.

**Sponsor:** Give me a second option that avoids showing unfinished analytics.

**PM:** Option 2 uses 8 h for a clearly labeled mocked-analytics demo: 152 + 8 = 160 h, leaving 80 h of W5-W10 capacity, with F09/F11/F12/F13/F16 (110 h) all at the week-14 pilot.

**Sponsor:** I choose option 1 for the open day.

**PM:** I will record F16 and F11 (54 h) for W10, F09/F12/F13 (56 h) for W14, keep Could work out until W11, and enter simulated decision DR-01 by the end of W4 (2026-10-24) before updating the baseline for W5.
